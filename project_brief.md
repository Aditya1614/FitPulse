# My agent: FitPulse (Personal Fitness & Workout Coach)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps fitness enthusiasts build personalized workout routines and track fitness progression with a catalog of exercises and training plans.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User fitness goals (strength, hypertrophy, endurance), injuries/limitations, experience level, available equipment (home gym, dumbbells, commercial gym), and workout history/logged stats.
- Tools: Exercise catalog search, workout routine generator, session logger, target heart rate & 1RM calculator.
- Catalog/UI: An exercise library (movement name, muscle group, difficulty, equipment) and routine plans rendered as A2UI cards and structured workout tables (exercise, sets, reps, rest interval).
- Image gen: Motivational visual cards, proper exercise posture guidance illustrations, or workout milestone completion badges.
- Sandbox: Target heart rate zone computation (Karvonen formula), one-rep max (1RM) estimations (Brzycki/Epley formulas), and BMI/TDEE caloric expenditure calculations.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for 1RM / heart rate zone / caloric burn calculations, workout routine export.
